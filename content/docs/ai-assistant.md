# The AI assistant

The AI assistant does the work for you — just type what you want in plain English and it handles the rest. Open **Assistant** from the sidebar to get started.

## What you can ask

Type requests the way you'd say them out loud, for example:

- "Create an invoice for Acme for 2 chairs at 5000"
- "Record a 10,000 payment on INV-00007"
- "Show overdue invoices this month"
- "Add a client called Zed"

## What it can do

The assistant can:

- Create and edit **quotations, invoices, clients, products, services, projects and expenses**
- **Convert quotes to invoices**
- **Record payments**
- **Answer questions** about your data
- Handle **bulk actions**, such as "create 10 products" in one go

## You stay in control

Before anything is saved, the assistant shows a **confirmation card** so you can review the details first. Nothing is created or changed without your OK.

## Settings

In the assistant **settings** you can:

- Switch individual capabilities **on or off**
- Add your own **custom instructions** to guide how it works

> Tip: if a capability is turned off, the assistant won't offer it — turn it back on in settings any time.

## Before you start

The assistant needs an AI provider connected first. See **Set up the AI assistant** to get it ready.
