# Reports

Reports give you a clear picture of how your business is doing — what you're earning, what you're spending and who still owes you money.

## Where to find them

Go to **Reports**. There are two main views: **Profit & Loss** and **Aging receivables**.

## Profit & Loss

See whether your business is making money over any period you choose.

It shows:

- **Total revenue** — everything you've billed
- **Money received** — what's actually been paid
- **Total expenses** — what you've spent
- **Gross profit** and **margin** — your profit and how healthy it is
- A **month-by-month breakdown** and a view of **expenses by category**

> Use the **date range** filter to focus on a month, quarter or year. You can also filter by **project** to see the numbers for a single job.

## Aging receivables

See who owes you money and how late they are, so you know exactly who to chase.

Unpaid invoices are grouped by how overdue they are:

- **Current / not due**
- **1–30 days** overdue
- **31–60 days** overdue
- **61–90 days** overdue
- **90+ days** overdue

## Export and statements

- **Export** any figures to a spreadsheet for your records or your accountant.
- Generate a **per-client statement** showing what an individual customer owes.

> Check **Aging receivables** weekly, and turn on automatic reminders so overdue invoices chase themselves — see the reminders guide.
