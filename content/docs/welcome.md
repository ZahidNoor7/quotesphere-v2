# Welcome to QuoteSphere

QuoteSphere is your all-in-one tool for **quoting clients, sending invoices, getting paid, and tracking your money** — without spreadsheets.

## What you can do

- **Quote** clients with professional estimates, then turn an approved quote into an invoice in one click.
- **Invoice** clients, track what's paid vs. outstanding, and send invoices by **PDF, email or WhatsApp**.
- **Get paid** — record payments and let the app chase overdue invoices for you automatically.
- **Track spending** with expenses (and scan paper bills with the camera).
- **See the big picture** with a live dashboard and profit & aging reports.
- **Work faster** with a built-in **AI assistant** — just type "create an invoice for Acme…".

## Finding your way around

Everything lives in the left sidebar:

- **Overview** — your Dashboard and the AI Assistant
- **Sales** — Clients, Projects, Invoices, Quotations, Expenses, Messaging
- **Catalog** — your Products and Services
- **Admin** — Reports, Settings, and this Help center

## Getting started

1. Set up your company details so they appear on your documents — see **Company & document settings**.
2. Follow the **Quick start** to send your first invoice in about 5 minutes.
3. When you're ready to send emails, chase payments automatically, or use the AI assistant, head to the **Setup & configuration** guides.

> **New here?** Start with the **Quick start (5 minutes)** guide next — it walks you through adding a client and creating your first invoice.

## Need help?

Every feature has its own guide in this Help center (use the menu on the left). The guides are written in plain English — no technical knowledge needed.
