# Company & document settings

Your **Settings** area is where you set up how QuoteSphere looks and behaves. Open it from **Settings** in the sidebar — it has several tabs across the top.

## General

The most important tab to fill in first. Set your:

- **Company name, logo, address, email and phone** — these appear on every invoice and quotation.
- **Default currency** and which currencies you accept.
- **Default tax** and **payment terms** (used to pre-fill new documents).
- **Number formats** — the prefixes for your documents (e.g. `INV`, `QT`, `EXP`), so invoices number as INV-00001, INV-00002, and so on.

## Documents (design)

Customize how your **invoice and quotation PDFs** look — colors, fonts, which details show, header/footer, and terms & conditions. Create different designs and set a default.

## Appearance

Switch the app's **theme** (light/dark and color) and layout density to your taste.

## Integrations

Connect outside services. Each has its own guide:

- **Email** (send documents & reminders) — *Set up email*
- **WhatsApp** (chat & send PDFs) — *Connect WhatsApp*
- **AI Assistant** (chat & bill scanning) — *Set up the AI assistant*
- **Cloudinary** (logo, images, bill photos) — *Image hosting*
- **Currency exchange** (live rates for multi-currency)

## Reminders

Turn on and configure **automatic payment reminders** — see *Automatic payment reminders*.

## Team

Invite colleagues and set their **role** (what they're allowed to do — view only, create, manage, or full admin).

## Profile

Your personal account — name, avatar and password.

## Audit log

A record of who created, changed or deleted things — useful for keeping track when you have a team.

> **Start here:** fill in **General** first so your company details and logo appear correctly on the documents you send.
