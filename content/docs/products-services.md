# Products & services

Build a catalog of the things you sell so you never have to retype them on every quote or invoice — and your pricing stays consistent.

## Products vs. services

- **Products** are physical goods you sell. Each one has a **name**, an optional **SKU** (your own product code), a **price**, a **stock quantity**, a **low-stock level**, a **category** and a **unit**.
- **Services** are work you do. Each one has a **name**, a **price**, a **category** and a **unit**.

## Add an item

1. Go to **Products** (or **Services**).
2. Click to add a new item and fill in its details.
3. Save.

> Set up your most common items once, up front. It makes quoting and invoicing far quicker and keeps your prices the same every time.

## How they help on quotes and invoices

- When you add a product or service as a line item on a quote or invoice, its **price fills in automatically** — no looking it up.
- Selling a tracked **product** on an invoice automatically **reduces its stock**. When stock runs low, the item is **flagged** so you know to reorder.

## Import many at once

Already have your catalog in a spreadsheet? You don't need to add items one by one.

1. Go to **Products** (or **Services**).
2. Click **Import**.
3. Upload your spreadsheet to bring everything in together.

> For step-by-step help preparing and uploading your file, see the **Importing** guide.
