# Quotations

A quotation is a price estimate you send a client before the work begins. This guide shows you how to create one and turn it into an invoice.

## Creating a quotation

1. Open **Quotations** and select **+ New quotation**.
2. Pick the **client** you're quoting.
3. Add your **line items** — for each one, enter a description, quantity, and unit price, or pick a saved product or service.
4. Set the **tax** (as a percentage or a fixed amount), any **discount**, and **delivery charges**.
5. Choose the **currency** and a **validity date** (how long the quote is good for).
6. Add any **notes** for the client.

Totals are calculated automatically as you go, so there's no math to do yourself. Each quotation gets an automatic number like **QT-00001**.

## Quotation status

A quotation moves through these stages:

- **Draft** — still being prepared
- **Pending** — sent and awaiting the client's decision
- **Approved** or **Rejected** — the client's response

> **Tip:** Quotes past their validity date are marked **expired** automatically, so you always know which estimates are still current.

## Sharing a quotation

Once your quote is ready, you can:

- Download it as a **PDF**
- Send it by **email**
- Send it on **WhatsApp**

## Convert to invoice

This is the time-saver. When a client approves a quote, select **Convert to invoice** to turn it into an invoice in a single click — no re-typing the client, line items, or totals.
