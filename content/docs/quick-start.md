# Quick start (5 minutes)

Let's send your first invoice. You'll add a client and create an invoice — that's the core of QuoteSphere.

## 1. Add your company details (1 min)

Go to **Settings → General** and fill in your **company name**, address, and upload a **logo**. This is what clients see at the top of your invoices and quotes. (Full guide: **Company & document settings**.)

## 2. Add a client (1 min)

1. Open **Clients** from the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add client**.
3. Enter a **name** and **phone** (required). Add an **email** too if you want to send invoices or reminders by email.
4. Click **Save**.

## 3. Create an invoice (2 min)

1. Open **Invoices** and click **+ New invoice**.
2. Choose the **client** you just added.
3. Add a **line item** — a description, quantity and price. Add as many as you need. (If you've set up Products/Services, you can just pick one and the price fills in.)
4. Optionally set **tax**, a **discount**, and a **due date**.
5. Click **Save**. Your invoice gets a number like **INV-00001**, and the total is calculated for you.

> **Tip:** Prefer to talk instead of click? Open the **Assistant** and type *"create an invoice for [client] for 2 chairs at 5000"* — it builds it and shows you a confirmation before saving.

## 4. Send it

From the invoice, download a **PDF**, or send it by **email** or **WhatsApp**. (Email and WhatsApp need a quick one-time setup — see the **Set up email** and **Connect WhatsApp** guides.)

## 5. Get paid

When the client pays, open the invoice and click **Record payment**. The outstanding balance updates automatically. (Guide: **Recording payments**.)

## What next?

- Turn on **Automatic payment reminders** so overdue invoices chase themselves.
- Set up the **AI assistant** to create documents (and scan bills) by chatting.
- Add your **Products & services** so quoting and invoicing get even faster.
