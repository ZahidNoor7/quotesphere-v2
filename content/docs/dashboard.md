# Your dashboard

The dashboard is your home screen — a snapshot of your whole business in one place. Open it to see where your money is and what needs your attention today.

## What the numbers tell you

At the top you'll see your headline figures:

- **Total revenue** — everything you've invoiced
- **Money received** — what your clients have actually paid
- **Total outstanding** — invoiced amounts still unpaid
- **Total expenses** — what you've spent

You'll also see counts of your **invoices**, **quotations** and **clients**, plus how many invoices are currently **overdue**.

## Charts and insights

Further down, the dashboard helps you spot trends:

- A **revenue-by-month** chart showing how your income moves over time
- Your **top clients** by value
- Your most **recent invoices**
- A breakdown of **payment status** (paid, unpaid, and so on)
- **Expenses by category**, so you can see where your money goes

## Alerts

The dashboard surfaces **alerts** for things that can't wait, such as:

- Invoices that are **overdue**
- Invoices **due in the next 7 days**
- Quotations that are **about to expire**

Click an alert to jump straight to the item that needs attention.

> Tip: start your day on the dashboard. A quick glance tells you who owes you money, what's due soon, and which quotes you should follow up before they expire.
