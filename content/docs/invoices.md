# Invoices

An invoice is a bill you send a client to get paid. This guide shows you how to create invoices and track what you're owed.

## Creating an invoice

1. Open **Invoices** and select **+ New invoice**.
2. Choose the **client** you're billing.
3. Add your **line items** — enter them yourself or pick saved products and services.
4. Set the **tax**, any **discount**, and **delivery charges**.
5. Choose the **currency** and a **due date**.
6. Add any **notes** for the client.

Each invoice is auto-numbered like **INV-00001**, and totals are calculated automatically.

> **Tip:** Always add a due date so overdue tracking and automatic reminders work.

## Tracking payments

The app keeps a running tally of how much is **paid** versus **outstanding**, along with a payment status that moves from **pending** to **partial** to **complete**. To enter money you've received, see the Recording payments guide.

## Sharing an invoice

Once ready, you can:

- Download it as a **PDF**
- Send it by **email**
- Send it on **WhatsApp**

## Stock and recurring invoices

- **Automatic stock updates:** If a line item is a tracked product, its stock count goes down automatically when you invoice it.
- **Recurring invoices:** Set a frequency — weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly — and the app automatically creates the next invoice for you. This is ideal for retainers, subscriptions, and regular billing.
