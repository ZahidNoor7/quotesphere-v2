# Team roles & permissions

Every person you add to your workspace has a **role**. The role decides what they can do — from view‑only access all the way up to full admin. You set a role when you add a member, and you can change it any time.

Open **Settings → Team** to manage your team. Only **admins** can add members, change roles, or remove people.

## The four roles at a glance

| What they can do | Viewer | Staff | Manager | Admin |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **View** everything — clients, quotations, invoices, expenses, projects, products & reports | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Create** records (new invoice, quotation, expense, client, project…) | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Edit** existing records | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Delete** records | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Manage the team** — add / remove members, change roles | — | — | — | ✓ |
| **Company settings & integrations** (branding, tax, WhatsApp, email, AI, etc.) | — | — | — | ✓ |

## What each role is for

- **Viewer** — read‑only. Perfect for an accountant, a partner, or anyone who needs to *see* your numbers and documents but shouldn't change anything.
- **Staff** — can view and **create** records, but can't edit or delete what already exists. Good for team members who raise quotations and invoices day‑to‑day.
- **Manager** — can view, create, **edit and delete** records. A manager runs the books but **can't** change company‑wide settings or manage the team.
- **Admin** — full control. Admins do everything a manager can, **plus** manage the team and change company settings and integrations. The person who created the workspace is an admin, and you can promote others to admin too.

## Adding a member

1. Go to **Settings → Team** and click **Add member**.
2. Enter their **name** and **email**, set a **password** (use the generate button for a strong one), and pick a **role**.
3. Click **Add member**. Share the email and password with them — they can sign in straight away.

> Tip: ask new members to change their password after their first sign‑in, from **Settings → Profile**.

## Changing a role or removing someone

On the **Team** list, admins can:

- **Change a role** — pick a new role from the dropdown next to a member; it takes effect immediately.
- **Remove a member** — click the trash icon and confirm. They lose access right away. Anything they created stays with your workspace — nothing is lost.

A couple of safe‑guards keep your workspace healthy:

- You **can't change your own role or remove yourself**, so an organization always keeps at least one admin.
- Roles and members are scoped to **your** workspace only. Members can **never** see or touch another organization's data — no matter their role.
