# WhatsApp messaging

Chat with your clients on WhatsApp without ever leaving QuoteSphere. Open **Messaging** to keep all your conversations in one place.

## Finding your way around

When you open **Messaging**, you'll see your WhatsApp conversations listed on the left and the selected chat on the right.

- On a phone, **tap a conversation** to open it.
- Use the **back arrow** to return to your list of conversations.

## Sending and receiving

- Send and receive **text messages** with your clients.
- Attach and send invoice or quotation **PDFs** right inside the chat.
- You can also send a document straight from an invoice or quotation.

## The 24-hour rule

WhatsApp has a rule you should know about: outside a **24-hour window**, a customer must message you first before you can message them. Once they reply, the window reopens and you can message freely again.

> Tip: pair messaging with automatic reminders to chase overdue invoices over WhatsApp — a friendly nudge often gets you paid faster.

## Before you start

This needs WhatsApp to be connected first. See **Connect WhatsApp** to set it up.
