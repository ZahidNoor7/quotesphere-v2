# Automatic payment reminders

Let QuoteSphere chase overdue (and soon-due) invoices for you, by **email and/or WhatsApp**, on a daily schedule. You set it up in **Settings → Reminders**.

## Before you start

Reminders send through a channel, so connect at least one first:

- **Email** — see **Set up email**
- **WhatsApp** — see **Connect WhatsApp**

> Until a channel is connected, its switch on the Reminders page stays locked, and you'll see a **Configure →** link that takes you to set it up. You can't turn reminders on with no channel.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Settings → Reminders**.
2. Switch **Automatic reminders** on.
3. Under **Channels**, turn on **Email**, **WhatsApp**, or both (only the ones you've connected can be switched on).
4. Set the timing:
   - **Remind before due (days)** — e.g. `3` sends a friendly nudge 3 days before the due date.
   - **Overdue reminders (days)** — e.g. `1, 7, 14` sends follow-ups 1, 7 and 14 days after the due date.
5. Click **Save**.

## How it works

- It runs **once a day** and only contacts clients about invoices that still have an **outstanding balance**.
- Each invoice gets **at most one reminder per day**, so clients are never spammed.
- Email reminders need the client to have an **email address** on their profile; WhatsApp reminders use the phone number on the invoice.

> **Tip:** Always set a **due date** on your invoices — reminders are based on it. And keep payments recorded, so paid invoices stop getting chased automatically.
