# Clients

Clients (sometimes called customers) are the people and companies you bill. This guide shows you how to add and manage them.

## Adding a client

1. Open **Clients** from the sidebar.
2. Select **Add client**.
3. Enter a **name** and **phone** — these are required.
4. Optionally add an email, company, address, tax ID, notes, preferred currency, and set the client as active or inactive.
5. Save the client.

> **Tip:** Add an email address so you can send invoices and automatic reminders. Add a phone number with the country code (for example, 9230…) so you can reach the client on WhatsApp.

## The client profile

Each client has its own profile page that brings everything together in one place. On it you'll see:

- Their invoices, quotations, and expenses
- Totals at a glance: **total invoiced**, **paid**, and **outstanding**

This makes it easy to see exactly where you stand with any client.

## Finding and organizing clients

- **Search** to quickly jump to a specific client.
- **Filter** your list to focus on the clients you need.
- **Edit** a client any time to update their details.

## Importing and exporting

- **Export** your client list to CSV or Excel to keep a copy or work with it in a spreadsheet.
- **Import** clients from a spreadsheet to add many at once — see the Importing guide for step-by-step help.

## Deleting clients

You can remove several clients at once using **bulk-delete**.

> **Warning:** If a client has linked invoices or quotations, the app will warn you before deleting so you don't lose connected records by mistake.
