# Expenses & bill scanning

Keep track of everything your business spends — vendor bills, supplier purchases and other costs — so you always know your true profit.

## Record an expense

1. Go to **Expenses** and click **+ Add expense**.
2. Enter the **bill date** and the **vendor name** (who you paid).
3. Add your **line items** — a description, quantity and unit price for each thing you bought.
4. Set the **tax**, any **discount** and the **currency**. The total adds up automatically as you go.
5. Click **Save expense**.

Every expense is given its own number, like **EXP-00001**, and a status that shows where it stands:

- **Draft** — started but not finalised
- **Recorded** — saved and counted
- **Verified** — checked and confirmed
- **Cancelled** — no longer applies

## Scan a bill (the easy way)

Instead of typing everything in by hand, let the app read your receipt for you.

1. On the expense form, click **Scan a bill**.
2. Take a photo or upload an image or PDF of the paper or digital receipt.
3. The AI assistant reads the **vendor**, **date** and **line items** and fills the form in for you.
4. Quickly review what it picked up, fix anything if needed, then click **Save expense**.

> Bill scanning relies on the AI assistant being switched on. If you don't see results, set it up first — see **Set up the AI assistant**.

## Find and manage expenses

From the **Expenses** list you can:

- Browse all your recorded expenses
- **Search** to find a specific bill or vendor
- **Delete** an expense you no longer need

> Expenses feed straight into your Profit & Loss report. The more diligently you record them, the more accurately you'll see what your business is really making.
