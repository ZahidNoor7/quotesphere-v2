# Recording payments

This is how you mark an invoice as paid, whether fully or in part. This guide shows you how to record money you've received.

## Recording a payment

1. Open the invoice you've been paid for.
2. Select **Record payment**.
3. Enter the **amount** received.
4. Choose the **method**: cash, bank transfer, card, online, or cheque.
5. Set the **date** of the payment.
6. Optionally add a **reference or note** (for example, a transaction number).
7. Save the payment.

The invoice's **outstanding** balance and **payment status** update instantly, moving from **pending** to **partial** to **complete**.

## Partial payments

You can record several partial payments over time as a client pays you in installments.

> **Note:** You can't record more than the outstanding amount, so the balance can never go below zero.

## Fixing a mistake

Entered a payment by accident? You can delete a payment to remove it and restore the correct balance.

> **Tip:** Keeping payments up to date makes your dashboard, the Aging report, and automatic reminders accurate. Reminders only chase invoices that still have a balance, so recording payments promptly stops clients being reminded about money they've already paid.
