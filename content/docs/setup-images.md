# Image hosting (Cloudinary)

QuoteSphere stores images and files — your **company logo**, **product photos**, **document attachments**, and the **bill photos** you scan into expenses — with a service called **Cloudinary**. Setting it up once enables all of those.

## Get your Cloudinary details

1. Go to **cloudinary.com** and create a free account.
2. Open your **Dashboard**. Near the top you'll see three values:
   - **Cloud name**
   - **API Key**
   - **API Secret**
3. Copy all three.

## Connect it in QuoteSphere

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Cloudinary** and turn it **on**.
2. Paste your **Cloud name**, **API key**, and **API secret**.
3. Click **Save**.

## What it's used for

- Uploading your **company logo** and **product images**.
- Attaching images to invoice/quotation line items.
- The **Scan a bill** feature on expenses (the photo you take is uploaded here, then read by the AI).

> **Good to know:** Cloudinary's free plan is generous and is plenty for most small businesses. Your credentials are stored securely in your settings.
